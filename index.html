Our Story

A family bakery bringing the recipes of Greece to Melbourne

Welcome to FILO & MELI

Nestled in the vibrant shopping district of Camberwell, FILO & MELI is a family-owned Greek bakery cafe dedicated to preserving the culinary heritage of Greece. Our name — Filo & Meli — honours the two ingredients at the heart of our craft: delicate, hand-rolled filo pastry and pure golden honey.

Every day, our bakers prepare traditional recipes passed down through generations. From flaky spanakopita to syrup-soaked baklava, each product is made with love, using only the finest ingredients. Whether you're joining us for a quick breakfast, a leisurely lunch, or an afternoon pick-me-up, you'll find a warm welcome and a true taste of the Mediterranean.
